Aurora Dynamics - Employee Handbook: Remote Work Policy

Aurora Dynamics uses a hybrid work model.

Engineering and product teams work from the office on Tuesdays and
Thursdays. The remaining days are flexible. Customer support in Lisbon
works fully on site because of the secure monitoring room requirement.

Employees may work fully remotely for up to 6 weeks per year, for example
to visit family in another city or country. This is called a "remote
stretch" and must be approved by a manager in Compass at least 1 week
before it starts.

Remote work outside the employee's country of employment is limited to
30 days per year for tax reasons.

Equipment:
Every employee receives a laptop and a 400 dollar one time budget for
home office equipment. The budget is claimed through Compass with
receipts.

Core hours:
All teams keep core hours from 11:00 to 15:00 in their local time zone.
Meetings across offices are scheduled inside a shared window of
13:00 to 17:00 India time.
