Aurora Dynamics - Q1 2025 Business Update (Internal)

This is an internal summary shared with all employees in April 2025.

Fleet growth:
At the end of Q1 2025, Aurora Dynamics had 1,830 Carrier X2 robots
deployed across 96 hospital campuses in 11 countries. That is up from
1,410 robots at the end of 2024.

Revenue:
Quarterly recurring revenue reached 15.9 million dollars, a 22 percent
increase over the previous quarter.

Reliability:
Average robot uptime was 99.1 percent. The most common cause of downtime
was blocked lidar sensors, which accounted for 38 percent of all support
tickets.

New business:
The company signed its largest contract to date with the Meridian Health
Network: 240 robots across 8 hospitals in Germany, rolling out through
2025 and 2026.

Hiring:
The company plans to grow from 214 to 260 employees by the end of 2025,
with most new roles in the Austin engineering office.

Priorities for Q2:
1. Reduce lidar related tickets by shipping the self cleaning lidar dome.
2. Launch the Carrier X2 Cold variant with a refrigerated compartment for
   blood and vaccine transport.
3. Complete the Meridian Health Network pilot in Munich.
