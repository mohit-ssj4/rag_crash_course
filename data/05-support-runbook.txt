Aurora Dynamics - Support Runbook: Common Carrier X2 Issues

This runbook is used by the Lisbon support team to resolve frequent
customer issues with the Carrier X2 robot.

Issue: robot stops in a corridor and blinks orange.
Meaning: the lidar sensor is blocked or dirty.
Fix: wipe the lidar dome with the microfiber cloth from the maintenance
kit. If the robot still blinks orange after cleaning, restart it by
holding the power button for 8 seconds. Escalate to level 2 support if
the issue repeats more than twice in one day.

Issue: robot cannot call the elevator.
Meaning: the elevator integration service has lost its network session.
Fix: open the fleet dashboard, select the robot, and press "Reconnect
building systems". Reconnection takes about 2 minutes. If it fails,
check that the hospital's integration gateway has power.

Issue: compartment will not open after badge scan.
Meaning: the badge reader cache is stale.
Fix: sync badges from the fleet dashboard using "Push badge list". The
sync completes in under 5 minutes. As a temporary measure, a nurse
supervisor can open the compartment with the override code from the
shift binder.

Issue: battery drains faster than usual.
Meaning: wheel brushes are worn or a wheel is misaligned, increasing
motor load.
Fix: schedule a DockBrush inspection. Robots with battery life below
10 hours must be rotated out of service.

Escalation: level 2 support is reachable at ext 4400, 24 hours a day.
