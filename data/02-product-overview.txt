Aurora Dynamics - Product Guide: Carrier X2

The Carrier X2 is the flagship delivery robot of Aurora Dynamics.

Key specifications:
- Payload capacity: 25 kilograms
- Top speed: 5.4 kilometers per hour indoors
- Battery life: 14 hours of continuous operation
- Charging time: 90 minutes on a fast dock, 4 hours on a standard dock
- Navigation: lidar plus six depth cameras, no floor markers required
- Compartments: two lockable compartments, each opened by staff badge scan

The Carrier X2 can call elevators on its own. It integrates with elevator
systems from three vendors: Kone, Otis, and Schindler. Integration with a
new elevator vendor typically takes six weeks.

The robot cleans its own wheels at the end of every shift using the
DockBrush station, which is sold separately.

Pricing: hospitals do not buy the robot outright. Aurora Dynamics charges
a subscription of 2,900 dollars per robot per month, which includes
maintenance, software updates, and 24/7 remote monitoring.

The Carrier X2 replaced the earlier Carrier X1 in 2023. The X1 is no
longer sold but is still supported until December 2026.
