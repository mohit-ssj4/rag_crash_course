Aurora Dynamics - Company Overview

Aurora Dynamics is a fictional robotics company used as the sample dataset
for this RAG crash course. Everything in these documents is made up.

Aurora Dynamics was founded in 2019 in Bengaluru, India, by Priya Raman and
Daniel Okafor. The company builds autonomous delivery robots for hospital
campuses. Its flagship product is the Carrier X2, a four-wheeled robot that
transports medicine, lab samples, and meals between hospital wards.

The company has 214 employees across three offices: the headquarters in
Bengaluru, an engineering office in Austin, Texas, and a customer support
hub in Lisbon, Portugal.

Aurora Dynamics is privately funded. Its most recent funding round was a
Series B of 48 million dollars, led by Meridian Growth Partners in
March 2024.

The company mission statement is: "Move critical supplies so caregivers
can stay with patients."
